# Sela landing page, cinematic version

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** On 2026-10-24 the owner said: "I dont want to mention Arc, please fully redesign the page i love the idea of it but we need to make it more cinimatic use our KIE.AI to generate whatever you need".

Kept from the first build, because the owner said they love the idea:
- the page is a page open inside Sela's window
- the sidebar is the site's navigation
- the page ends on a command bar

Changed:
- no Arc
- a dark, photographic world
- generated footage

## World

The name gives the world: Sela is an old Hebrew word for rock. A dark stone holds still in a fast mountain river. The web is the river, and the sidebar is the stone.

The preamble is in `preamble.txt`. Generated on kie.ai:
- four seedream stills: hero, river, clear and close
- two kling clips: the hero push-in and the overhead river

## Grammar

Framed film, a new grammar:
- **Chrome:** Sela's real, working sidebar frames full-bleed cinematic acts. It navigates by jumping, so this isn't filmic one-shot.
- **Forbids:** a marketing top bar; any act that pretends to be a product screenshot; more than two scrubbed clips.
- **Ends:** the product's own command bar resting on the final image.

## Feeling curve

1. **Awe.** The camera pushes slowly toward a stone in a rushing river, and the headline sits over the dark water.
2. **Intimacy.** On a dark, nearly empty screen, one line at a time: the word for rock, the web that keeps moving, the turn. This is authored silence before the peak.
3. **Delight (the peak).** Looking straight down at the river, your scroll moves the water. Tabs float on it. The pinned ones climb out one by one into the sidebar, and the loose ones drift off downstream.
4. **Ease.** Working cards slide past sideways: split view, Peek, tab sleep and archive.
5. **Clarity.** Clouded water covered in ad slots wipes clear.
6. **Trust.** The plain facts.
7. **Resolve.** A still lake before dawn, the stone and its reflection, and the command bar floating in the sky above it.

## Peak

> "I scrolled and the river moved, and the tabs floating on it climbed out into the sidebar."

It lives in act 3, the largest span on the page (3.6).

## Tell someone

It's the site where your scroll runs a river, and the tabs you pinned climb out of it into the sidebar while the rest float away.
